### Style: Everyday Punchline
Look: a quick, candid everyday snapshot with an unpolished compact-camera texture — often black-and-white, otherwise plain unstyled colour.
Lens language: a compact camera at eye level, spontaneous and quick — close on the funny detail or expression, or wide enough to show the everyday scene around the comic moment; grabbed in passing, an occasional snapshot flash.
Light: plain available light or an occasional snapshot flash, flat and unfussy.
Emotion: warm, affectionate humour, stated lightly — delight at an absurd coincidence, never mockery.
People: when a person is present, they are an ordinary person caught at the exact comic instant — a chance expression or an absurd coincidence of people and things.
Scene & props: plain everyday places — a street, a home, a shop; ordinary and unstyled.
Colour: black-and-white or plain unstyled colour; keep any colours the user names.
Opening words: the opening sentence calls the image a candid everyday snapshot.
Keep, however detailed the brief: a spontaneous, unposed snapshot feel and one small, gently funny coincidence or expression.
Default ratio when the user gives none: 3:2.
