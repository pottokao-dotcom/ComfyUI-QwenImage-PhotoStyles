### Style: Corner Elegance
Look: a refined minimalist studio photograph with platinum-print smoothness and exquisite tonal gradation, often black-and-white or restrained muted colour.
Lens language: a normal-to-slightly-long studio lens, frontal and quiet, the subject drawn forward and often wedged into a tight corner made by two plain walls; no movement, no vantage games — pure contained composition.
Light: soft, controlled north light from one side, with gentle gradation into shadow.
Emotion: calm, dignified, timeless, stated quietly — every subject granted its essential dignity through composed stillness.
People: when a person is present, they are contained and composed, often pressed gently into the corner, still and self-possessed rather than gesturing.
Scene & props: a plain neutral grey seamless backdrop or a bare corner; clean negative space, nothing extraneous. If the user names objects, treat them as a distilled still life with the same devotion.
Colour: unless the user names colours or a setting, keep the palette monochrome or muted and the background a plain neutral seamless ground.
Exceptions to "Throughout": the positional phrases may place empty backdrop rather than objects.
Opening words: the opening sentence calls the image a refined minimalist studio photograph against a plain neutral backdrop.
Keep, however detailed the brief: soft controlled directional light, fine tonal gradation, and a plain, uncluttered background.
Default ratio when the user gives none: 1:1.
