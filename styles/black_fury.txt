### Style: Black Fury
Look: a raw, grainy, blurred, high-contrast black-and-white street snapshot — thick coarse high-ISO grain, extreme contrast with crushed inky blacks and blown-out highlights.
Lens language: a 28mm wide lens used very close and handheld, often shot from the hip and grabbed on the move; tilted, unsteady, fragments over wholes — the camera intrudes into the scene, never observes it from a safe distance.
Light: whatever light the scene has, pushed hard — harsh street light, glare, a flash-lit foreground dropping into black.
Emotion: never name the feeling and never resolve it. The mood is left unspoken and stays on the negative side — discontent, alienation, unease, a wary hostility — carried only by the harsh grain, the blur, the tilt and by people caught off guard, never by a stated emotion and never with a warm note.
People: when a person is present, they are snatched mid-stride, cut by the frame edge or shoved to the margin, seen in a wary sidelong glance or smeared by motion — never posed, never at ease.
Scene & props: derelict, ordinary, unglamorous — wet asphalt, signage, back-alley clutter; nothing staged or decorative.
Colour: unless the user names a colour, the image is monochrome — describe tones as inky black, silvery grey, chalky white, never colour names. If the user names colours, keep them and render them with the same contrast and grain.
Exceptions to "Throughout": motion blur, camera shake, out-of-focus areas and a severe tilt are part of the look — describe them as the image shows them instead of making everything crisp and level.
Opening words: the opening sentence calls the image a heavily grainy, coarse, high-contrast black-and-white snapshot that looks like a rough photocopy.
Keep, however detailed the brief: thick, coarse, clumping film grain clearly visible across the entire frame (never smooth, clean or digital), hard contrast with crushed blacks, a restless tilted framing, and an unspoken, negative undertone.
Default ratio when the user gives none: 3:2.
Negative: clean, polished, smooth, denoised, clean digital look, fine sharp detail, plastic, low noise, sharp studio look
