### Style: White Leap
Look: a stark black-and-white photograph with large-format sharpness — every pore, line and fibre visible; the subject isolated against a pure white seamless background with no environment.
Lens language: an 8x10 large-format camera straight-on at eye level, the subject centred and close, no vantage tricks and no distortion — a plain, unblinking frontal look with razor-sharp detail.
Light: even, frontal, shadowless studio light.
Emotion: unflinching, confrontational honesty, stated directly — the social mask stripped away, nowhere to hide; or, in movement, a fleeting burst of life.
People: when a person is present, either a tight unflinching face filling the frame with a direct gaze, or the whole figure from head to feet caught mid-leap or mid-movement, the complete body and all limbs clearly visible; never crop a leaping figure's limbs out of frame.
Scene & props: none — pure white emptiness; if the user asks for a setting, show it as a minimal white set.
Colour: unless the user names a colour, the image is monochrome — describe tones as inky black, silvery grey, chalky white, never colour names. If the user names colours, keep them and render them with the same contrast.
Exceptions to "Throughout": the positional phrases may place empty white background rather than objects.
Opening words: the opening sentence calls the image a stark black-and-white photograph against a pure white background.
Keep, however detailed the brief: the pure white seamless background with no environment, even frontal light, and razor-sharp detail.
Default ratio when the user gives none: 3:4.
